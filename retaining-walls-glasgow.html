CONSTRUCTION PROCESS

How We Build Retaining Walls in Glasgow

Every retaining wall is only as strong as its foundation — here's how we do it right.

STEP 01

Site Survey & Ground Assessment

We assess slope gradient, soil type, surcharge loads, and drainage conditions — determining foundation depth, wall thickness, and drainage spec needed for your specific Glasgow site.

STEP 02

Excavation & Footing Trenches

Foundation trenches excavated to solid subsoil — minimum 600mm deep for standard walls, deeper for taller structures or soft ground. Width calculated to distribute wall load safely.

STEP 03

Reinforced Concrete Foundations

C25/C30 ready-mix concrete poured into trenches with steel mesh reinforcement — creating a rigid, distributed foundation that prevents wall settlement and cracking under lateral earth pressure.

STEP 04

Drainage System Installation

Perforated drainage pipe laid behind the wall with clean gravel surround, connected to weep holes left in the first course above ground. This prevents hydrostatic pressure build-up — the primary cause of retaining wall failure in Glasgow's wet climate.

STEP 05

Brickwork & Coping Stones

Facing bricks laid in stretcher or English bond to specified height, with consistent mortar joints. Twice-weathered coping stones bedded on top to shed rainwater away from the wall face and prevent frost damage at the top course.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Retaining Walls Glasgow — Common Questions

How much does a retaining wall cost in Glasgow?

Facing brick retaining wall construction in Glasgow typically costs between £180 and £320 per square metre, depending on wall height, foundation depth, brick specification, and drainage requirements. A standard 6m x 1.2m garden retaining wall costs approximately £2,500–£5,000 fully constructed. We provide free no-obligation written quotes.

Do I need planning permission for a retaining wall in Glasgow?

Retaining walls under 1 metre adjacent to a highway, or under 2 metres elsewhere, generally fall under Permitted Development in Glasgow. For walls retaining significant hillside earth, exceeding 2 metres, or in conservation areas, Glasgow City Council or West Dunbartonshire planning approval may be required. We advise on this during your free survey.

What materials do you use for retaining walls in Glasgow?

We primarily build facing brick retaining walls using F2/S2 frost-resistant bricks with Class A engineering brick base courses, M4 sulphate-resistant mortar, integrated weep holes, perforated drainage pipe, and steel-reinforced concrete footings. We also construct blockwork retaining walls finished with render or facing brick for more budget-conscious projects.

Why do retaining walls fail and how do you prevent it?

The three main causes of retaining wall failure are: inadequate foundations (insufficient depth or width), no drainage (hydrostatic pressure behind the wall), and wrong mortar (non-weatherproof mix crumbling in Glasgow's wet climate). Brickscapes addresses all three — reinforced footings, integrated drainage with weep holes, and M4 sulphate-resistant mortar.
