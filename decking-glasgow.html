COMPOSITE VS TIMBER

Choosing the Right Decking for Glasgow's Climate

Both composite and timber have their place — here's how they compare for Scottish gardens.

Composite Decking

RECOMMENDED FOR GLASGOW
  • Lasts 25–30 years in Scottish weather
  • Zero annual maintenance (no oiling/staining)
  • Splinter-free — safe for children & pets
  • Anti-slip grooved surface when wet
  • Resistant to moss, algae, and staining
  • Higher upfront cost, far lower lifetime cost

Timber Decking

CLASSIC NATURAL OPTION
  • Lower initial installation cost
  • Warm, natural wood aesthetics
  • Ideal for period & traditional properties
  • Requires annual oiling or staining
  • Can become slippery & mossy when wet
  • Shorter 10–15 year practical lifespan
OUR INSTALLATION PROCESS

How We Install Decking in Glasgow

A proper subframe is the foundation of a deck that lasts decades. Here's our proven step-by-step process.

STEP 01

Free Site Survey

Joe Church visits your property to assess ground conditions, slopes, access, drainage, and your requirements — then provides a detailed, itemised written quote at no charge.

STEP 02

Ground Preparation

We clear and level the ground, remove existing turf or paving, and install concrete pad foundations or ground screw anchors for a level, solid deck base — critical for Scotland's soft ground conditions.

STEP 03

Subframe Construction

We construct a structural joist framework using C16 pressure-treated timber or galvanised steel bearers, fixed to post anchors and levelled precisely — the most important stage of any quality deck build.

STEP 04

Deck Board Installation

Composite or timber boards are laid perpendicular or at 45° to the frame using stainless steel hidden clip fixings for a seamless, screw-free surface. Boards are gapped for natural drainage between each run.

STEP 05

Finishing & Balustrade

Fascia boards, edge trim, steps, balustrade posts, and infill panels are fitted and finished. The site is cleared and we walk you through care and maintenance guidance before handover.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Composite Decking Glasgow — Common Questions

How much does composite decking cost in Glasgow?

Composite decking in Glasgow is typically priced between £150 and £250 per square metre fully installed, including structural subframe, composite boards, and hidden fixings. A 20m² composite garden deck costs approximately £3,000–£5,000 depending on complexity. We provide detailed, no-obligation written quotes after a free site visit.

Is composite decking slippery when wet in Glasgow?

Quality composite boards have a grooved or embossed anti-slip surface texture that provides excellent grip even in wet conditions — far superior to smooth timber boards. We only install composite boards that meet UK slip resistance standards, ensuring your deck is safe through Glasgow's wet autumn and winter months.

Do I need planning permission for a deck in Glasgow?

Most garden decks in Glasgow fall under Permitted Development — no planning permission needed if the deck is under 300mm above ground and covers less than 50% of the garden. Raised decks, decks in conservation areas, or near the front boundary may require Glasgow City Council approval. We advise on all planning requirements during our free survey.

Do you install decking in Clydebank and surrounding areas?

Yes — Brickscapes installs composite and timber decking across Clydebank (G81), Bearsden, Milngavie, Dumbarton, East Kilbride, Newton Mearns and all of West Scotland. Call Joe on +44 7884 248274 to arrange a free on-site survey.

How long does it take to install a garden deck in Glasgow?

A standard 20–30m² composite garden deck typically takes 2–4 days to install, depending on ground preparation required, subframe complexity, and any balustrade or lighting elements. Larger multi-level decks or those requiring concrete foundations may take 5–7 days. We provide clear timescales in your written quote.
