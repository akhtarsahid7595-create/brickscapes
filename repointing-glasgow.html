Brick & Sandstone Repointing in Glasgow & Clydebank

Protecting Glasgow Homes from Damp with Expert Repointing

Glasgow's heavy rainfall and freeze-thaw winters are relentless on external mortar joints. Cracked, crumbling, or recessed pointing is not just cosmetic — it allows water to penetrate walls, causing internal damp, spalling brickwork, and costly structural damage. At Brickscapes Limited, Joe Church's time-served bricklayers diagnose and repair deteriorated pointing before it becomes a major problem.

We repoint all types of masonry found in Glasgow and West Scotland — from Victorian sandstone tenements requiring NHL lime mortar to modern facing brick walls needing sulphate-resistant cement-based mixes. Every joint is raked back cleanly before new mortar is pressed and finished to the correct profile.

Warning Signs Your Property Needs Repointing

Mortar joints crumbling or falling out when touched
Gaps or recession of 5mm or more from brick face
Internal damp patches appearing after heavy rain
White salt staining (efflorescence) on brick faces
Chimney mortar disintegrating at the stack
REPOINTING SERVICES

All Types of Repointing We Carry Out in Glasgow

From chimney stacks to garden walls — specialist pointing and mortar repair for every masonry type.

Brick Repointing Glasgow

Standard facing brick repointing using sulphate-resistant cement-lime mortar (1:1:6 mix). Joints raked to 15mm depth, cleaned, and repacked with weatherstruck, flush, or bucket-handle finish to match original pointing profile.

Victorian Sandstone Repointing

Specialist lime mortar repointing for Glasgow's Victorian and Edwardian sandstone tenements. We use NHL 3.5 or NHL 5 natural hydraulic lime — never hard OPC cement, which traps moisture and causes sandstone spalling.

Chimney Repointing Glasgow

Chimney stack and pot repointing at height across Glasgow. Deteriorated chimney pointing is one of the most common sources of roof-level water ingress. We repoint chimney stacks, rebedding pot cowls, and repoint flaunching where required.

Garden & Retaining Wall Repointing

External garden wall, boundary wall, and retaining wall repointing across Glasgow and Clydebank. Waterproof mortar mixes used for below-ground and exposed wall sections to prevent saturation and frost attack.

Lime Mortar Pointing

Traditional lime mortar pointing for period properties, conservation areas, and Listed Buildings across Glasgow. Breathable, flexible lime mortars matched to original joint colour and profile — compliant with Historic Environment Scotland guidance.

OUR REPOINTING PROCESS

How We Carry Out Brick Repointing in Glasgow

Every job done properly — raked back, cleaned, and packed with the right mortar for your masonry type.

STEP 01

Site Survey & Mortar Sample

We inspect all pointing, identify failed joints, and take a mortar sample to match the original colour, strength, and profile — essential for period sandstone properties.

STEP 02

Joint Raking

Failed mortar is carefully raked back to a minimum 15mm depth using angle grinders or hand tools — never chased too deeply, which risks brick damage.

STEP 03

Cleaning & Dampening

Joints are brushed clean of all loose debris and dust, then slightly dampened to prevent the fresh mortar from drying too fast and cracking — particularly important in Glasgow's drier summer periods.

STEP 04

Mortar Application

Fresh mortar is pressed firmly into joints in layers, ensuring full compaction with no voids — applied in horizontal beds first, then verticals, allowing proper curing between coats for deep joints.

STEP 05

Finishing & Clean Down

Joints are finished to the specified profile (weatherstruck, bucket-handle, or flush), mortar smears cleaned from brick faces, and the site left clean — leaving your walls weathertight and looking sharp.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Repointing Glasgow — Common Questions

How much does repointing cost in Glasgow?

Brick repointing in Glasgow typically costs £20–£45 per square metre, depending on accessibility and mortar type. Chimney repointing generally ranges from £350 to £900 depending on stack height and size. We provide free no-obligation surveys and written quotes for all repointing work in Glasgow and Clydebank.

When does brickwork need repointing?

Brickwork typically needs repointing when mortar joints show cracking, crumbling, or recession of more than 5–6mm from the brick face. In Scotland's wet climate, failed pointing allows water ingress which accelerates brick damage and causes internal damp. Most external brickwork needs repointing every 20–30 years, though exposed chimney stacks can fail sooner.

Do you repoint sandstone as well as brick in Glasgow?

Yes — we specialise in Victorian sandstone repointing using correctly formulated lime mortar. Using hard cement mortar on sandstone causes spalling and irreversible damage. Our time-served bricklayers use NHL 3.5 or 5 natural hydraulic lime to match the original pointing profile and protect Glasgow's historic sandstone buildings.

Can I repoint brickwork myself in Glasgow?

DIY repointing is possible on small, accessible areas, but incorrect mortar strength or poor joint raking can cause more damage than it prevents — particularly on sandstone or period masonry. For chimney stacks, scaffold or tower access is required for safety. We recommend a professional assessment before attempting any repointing on Glasgow's older stone buildings.
