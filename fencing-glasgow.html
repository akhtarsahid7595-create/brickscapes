HOW WE INSTALL FENCING

Our Fencing Installation Process in Glasgow

Proper post setting and solid foundations are what make fencing last — here's our approach.

STEP 01

Free Site Survey

Joe visits your Glasgow property to measure the fence run, check ground conditions, assess existing post locations, and recommend the best fence type and height for your needs and budget.

STEP 02

Old Fence Removal

We remove and dispose of old fence panels, posts, and gravel boards — clearing the boundary line ready for the new installation. Old concrete post footings are broken out where necessary.

STEP 03

Post Hole Digging

Fence post holes are dug to a minimum 600mm depth (deeper for taller fences or soft ground). Correct depth prevents posts leaning in Glasgow's wind and wet conditions — a common cause of early fence failure.

STEP 04

Posts Concreted In

Concrete posts are set plumb in mixed concrete — not postcrete bags. We check for level and plumb as we go, using temporary bracing to hold posts in position whilst concrete cures over 24 hours.

STEP 05

Panels & Gravel Boards Fitted

Gravel boards are fitted first at ground level, then fence panels are slotted and secured between posts. Post caps fitted to all timber posts. Site left clean — all offcuts and packaging removed.

Fencing Glasgow — Common Questions

What types of fencing do you install in Glasgow?

We install all common garden fence types including close board fencing, lap panel fencing, feather edge fencing, composite fencing, trellis, and picket fencing across Glasgow and Clydebank. We can also combine brick piers with timber or composite infill panels for a premium boundary finish.

How much does garden fencing cost in Glasgow?

Garden fencing in Glasgow costs approximately £90–£130 per metre run for 6ft close board or lap panel fencing, supply and fit including concrete posts and gravel boards. Composite fencing is typically £150–£250 per metre due to higher material cost. We provide free written quotes — call Joe on +44 7884 248274.

Do you supply and fit fence panels in Clydebank?

Yes — Brickscapes supply and fit fence panels across Clydebank (G81), Hardgate, Old Kilpatrick, Bearsden and all surrounding West Scotland areas. We source quality fence panels from trusted UK suppliers and install on new concrete posts with gravel boards for long-lasting performance.

Do I need planning permission for a fence in Glasgow?

In most cases, no planning permission is needed for a garden fence under 2 metres in height (1 metre adjacent to a road). This falls under Permitted Development rights. However, if your property is in a conservation area or is a listed building, additional consent may be required from Glasgow City Council.
