TYPES OF GARDEN WALLS

Garden Wall Options for Glasgow Homes

Boundary & Front Garden Walls

Single or double-skin brick boundary walls defining your property's front or side boundary. Constructed on concrete strip foundations with DPC, finished with coping stones — built to last decades in Glasgow's weather.

Gate Piers & Brick Pillars

Decorative brick gate piers and garden pillars — the finishing touch for driveways and front gardens across Glasgow. Built on independent concrete pad foundations with metal post bolts or hinges set into the brickwork during construction.

Raised Planters & Garden Beds

Brick-built raised garden planters and flower bed walls that add structure and visual interest to Glasgow gardens. Built with proper weep holes for drainage and lined with damp-proof membrane to protect the brickwork from soil moisture.

Garden Wall Repairs

Collapsed sections rebuilt, leaning walls underpinned, coping stones re-bedded, and failed pointing replaced across Glasgow. We assess existing walls and advise honestly — repair or full rebuild — giving you the right solution for your budget.

Coping Stone Replacement

Twice-weathered concrete or natural stone coping stones supplied and fitted across Glasgow. Coping stones shed rainwater away from the wall face and prevent frost damage at the top course — a critical detail often neglected on older Glasgow garden walls.

Brick Piers with Fence Panels

Combined brick pier and fence panel boundary installations — a premium finish combining the durability of masonry piers with the versatility of timber or composite infill panels. A popular upgrade for Glasgow homeowners looking to replace old fence posts.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Garden Walls Glasgow — Common Questions

How much does a garden wall cost in Glasgow?

A standard single-skin brick garden wall in Glasgow costs approximately £120–£200 per linear metre, including concrete strip foundation, brickwork, and coping stones. Double-skin boundary walls cost £200–£350 per linear metre. Prices vary depending on wall height, brick type, and ground conditions. All quotes are free and no-obligation.

Do you build garden walls in Clydebank?

Yes — Brickscapes builds garden walls, boundary walls, and decorative brick walls throughout Clydebank (G81), Hardgate, Old Kilpatrick, Bearsden, and all Glasgow areas. We offer free on-site surveys and written quotes with no obligation. Call Joe on +44 7884 248274.

Do I need planning permission for a garden wall in Glasgow?

In most cases, no planning permission is needed for garden walls under 1 metre adjacent to a road, or under 2 metres elsewhere — these fall under Permitted Development. However, properties in conservation areas or tenement properties may require Glasgow City Council consent. We advise on this during your free survey.

What is the best brick for a garden wall in Scotland?

For garden walls in Scotland's climate, we recommend F2/S2 frost-resistant facing bricks — rated for high water absorption environments and freeze-thaw cycling. Combined with M4 sulphate-resistant mortar and properly-seated coping stones, these walls perform reliably through Glasgow's wet winters year after year.
