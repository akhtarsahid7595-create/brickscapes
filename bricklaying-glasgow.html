FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Bricklaying & Wall Construction FAQs

How much does a brick retaining wall cost per metre in Glasgow?

Facing brick retaining wall installation in Glasgow typically ranges between £180 and £320 per square metre. Total project pricing depends on foundation excavation depth, brick selection (standard facing vs Class A engineering), coping stone choices, and site access.

Do I need planning permission for a garden retaining wall in West Dunbartonshire or Glasgow?

Garden walls under 1 metre near a road, or under 2 metres elsewhere, generally fall under Permitted Development in Glasgow City Council and West Dunbartonshire. For structural walls retaining heavy hillside earth or exceeding 2 metres, structural engineering approval may be required.

Why are weep holes necessary in retaining brick walls?

Weep holes are small vertical openings left in the bottom brick course to allow trapped ground water to drain out. Without weep holes, hydrostatic pressure from wet soil can crack or push over brick retaining walls during heavy Scottish winter rains.
