Frequently Asked Trade Questions

How much does a facing brick retaining wall cost per metre in Glasgow?

Facing brick retaining wall construction in Glasgow typically ranges between £180 and £320 per square metre. Total cost depends on excavation depth, engineering brick base specification, coping stone selection, and site access.

Do I need planning permission for a retaining wall in Glasgow or West Dunbartonshire?

Garden retaining walls under 1 metre near a road, or under 2 metres elsewhere, fall under Permitted Development in Glasgow and West Dunbartonshire. For structural walls retaining heavy hillside earth or exceeding 2 metres, structural engineering approval may be required.

Why are weep vents essential in retaining brickwork?

Weep vents allow ground water behind the wall to escape freely. Without weep vents, hydrostatic water pressure behind the wall can cause structural cracking or collapse during freeze-thaw winter cycles.
