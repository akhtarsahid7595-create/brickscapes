Dangers of Neglect

Why Clean Your Roof & Chimney?

Neglecting standard maintenance can lead to severe hazards, structural failure, and expensive repairs. Protect your home and family.

⚠

Risks of a Dirty Roof

  • Moss & Algae Damage: Moss traps dampness, degrading roof tiles and causing structural wood decay.
  • Leaks & Water Damage: Water seeps through cracked shingles, creating interior ceilings leaks.
  • Clogged Gutters: Loose debris drains down into gutters, resulting in overflow water damage.
  • Roof Fire Hazard: Accumulations of dry leaf litter form immediate fire hazards.
  • Poorer Insulation: Excess moisture reduces heating efficiency, raising monthly utility bills.
⚠

Risks of a Dirty Chimney

  • Creosote Chimney Fires: Highly flammable wood byproducts accumulate and spark fires inside flues.
  • Carbon Monoxide: Soot blockages deflect odorless, toxic gases back down into your living spaces.
  • Reduced Draft Airflow: Heavy residue chokes oxygen flow, causing low efficiency fireplace heating.
  • Expensive Repairs: Chimney structural failures require costly rebuild services from masonry teams.
