Dangers of Neglect

Why Clean Your Roof & Pavement?

Neglecting standard maintenance can lead to severe hazards, structural decay, and expensive repairs. Protect your home and family.

⚠

Risks of a Dirty Roof

  • Moss & Algae Damage: Moss and tropical algae trap dampness, degrading roof tiles and causing structural decay.
  • Leaks & Water Damage: Water seeps through cracked shingles, creating interior ceilings and wall leaks.
  • Clogged Gutters: Loose debris drains down into gutters, resulting in overflow water damage to your fascia and walls.
  • Reduced Property Value: Unchecked mold and dark water stains ruin curb appeal and lower property resale value.
⚠

Risks of Dirty Pavements

  • Severe Slipping Hazards: Black algae and mold growth on wet concrete/pavers make walkways extremely slick and dangerous.
  • Weed & Moss Growth: Plant roots break through paver joints, displacing support sand and causing pavers to shift.
  • Concrete Degradation: Acidic mold and trapped grime slowly corrode pavement surfaces, causing pitting and cracks.
  • Permanent Staining: Oil spills and carbon grime bind to porous surfaces, leaving unsightly permanent marks.
