Frequently Asked Questions in Bearsden

Do I need planning permission for a retaining wall in Bearsden (East Dunbartonshire)?

In Bearsden and East Dunbartonshire Council, garden retaining walls under 1 metre near a road, or under 2 metres elsewhere, generally fall under Permitted Development. For structural walls retaining heavy hillside earth, structural engineering calculations may be required.
