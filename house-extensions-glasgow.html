House Extension & Conversion Services in Glasgow

From single-storey kitchen extensions to integral garage conversions — full structural solutions across West Scotland.

Single-Storey Rear Extensions Glasgow

Full structural construction of single-storey rear extensions for Glasgow homes, including excavation of strip foundations, cavity wall brickwork in matching facing brick, internal blockwork leaf, steel beam installation for rear openings, and DPC/DPM installation. The most popular home improvement in Glasgow for kitchen and living room expansions.

Garage Conversions Glasgow

Full conversion of integral and attached garages into habitable rooms — home offices, playrooms, bedrooms, or utility rooms. We handle the structural masonry work including front wall infill brickwork, steel lintel installation, raising floor levels if required, and cavity closure. Garage conversions offer the best value for space in Glasgow per pound spent.

Loft Conversions Glasgow

Structural masonry work for loft conversions including gable wall extensions, dormer cheek brickwork, and structural alterations to support new floor joists. We work from structural engineer drawings and coordinate closely with the main build contractor to deliver the masonry element on programme and to specification.

Two-Storey Extensions Glasgow

Full structural masonry construction for two-storey side and rear extensions, delivering both ground-floor and first-floor additional accommodation in a single build programme. Two-storey extensions provide the greatest additional floor area per £1 of construction cost, with proportionally lower cost per m² than single-storey equivalents.

Steel Beam & RSJ Installation Glasgow

Supply and installation of structural steel beams (RSJs) for wall removals, wide rear opening creation, and load-bearing lintel replacements across Glasgow extensions and conversions. We work from structural engineer beam specifications and carry out all associated masonry work, padstone installation, and temporary propping to Building Regulations standards.

Planning & Building Regulations Advice

EXTEND VS MOVE

Why Extend Instead of Moving House in Glasgow?

The financial case for extending is stronger than ever in Glasgow's current property market.

Stamp Duty Savings

Moving to a larger Glasgow home attracts Stamp Duty Land Tax on the full purchase price. A £50,000 extension avoids tens of thousands in SDLT, legal fees, and estate agent costs associated with a property move.

Property Value Increase

A well-designed house extension typically adds 10–20% to Glasgow property values. A properly built garage conversion can add £15,000–£40,000 in value — often more than the cost of the conversion itself in higher-value areas like Bearsden and Newton Mearns.

Stay in Your Community

Extending allows you to stay in Clydebank, Bearsden, or wherever your children go to school, your neighbours are established, and your commute is set — without the disruption and risk of a full house move in an uncertain property market.

Designed for You

An extension is designed precisely for your family's needs — whether that's an open-plan kitchen and dining space, a ground floor bedroom for elderly relatives, or a home office for hybrid working. You specify the layout, the finish, and the features.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

House Extensions Glasgow — Common Questions

How much does a house extension cost in Glasgow?

A single-storey rear extension in Glasgow typically costs between £1,500–£2,500 per square metre. A 20–25m² kitchen extension costs approximately £35,000–£60,000 fully built and finished including structural masonry, roofing, glazing, and internal fit-out. We provide detailed written quotes for the masonry element after a free site consultation.

How much does a garage conversion cost in Glasgow?

Garage conversions in Glasgow typically cost £8,000–£20,000 depending on garage size and finish level. An integral garage conversion to a home office or bedroom, including masonry infill, insulation, plastering, electrics, and floor, typically costs £12,000–£18,000 for a standard single garage. Garage conversions provide excellent value — often costing less than a modest extension while adding equivalent usable space.

Do I need planning permission for a house extension in Glasgow?

Many single-storey rear extensions fall under Permitted Development — no full planning permission required, subject to size limits (typically 4m deep for detached houses, 3m for semi-detached). Conservation Areas, Listed Buildings, and extensions exceeding PD limits require full Glasgow City Council planning permission. We advise on your specific property's planning position during our free consultation.

Do you handle the complete extension build?

We specialise in the structural masonry element of house extensions — foundations, cavity wall brickwork in matching brick, blockwork, DPC, RSJ installation, and lintels. We work from architect's drawings and coordinate with your roofer, electrician, and plumber, or refer you to trusted trades partners across Glasgow if required.
