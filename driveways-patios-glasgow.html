PAVING WORKFLOW

Step-by-Step Driveway & Patio Installation

STEP 01

Deep Excavation

Excavating topsoil to 200mm-250mm depth to reach stable load-bearing sub-grade earth.

STEP 02

Sub-Base Compaction

Laying heavy geotextile membrane and compacting 150mm MOT Type 1 crushed stone with a Wacker plate.

STEP 03

Kerb Edging & Drainage

Haunching concrete edging kerbs and connecting ACO channel drains to soakaways under SuDS rules.

STEP 04

Paving Laying & Bonding

Laying slabs on a full wet mortar bed with SBR primer slurry coating on slab undersides.

STEP 05

Grouting & Surface Jointing

Filling joints with polymeric jointing sand or resin grout for weed-free, weatherproof joints.

PAVING FAQS

Driveway & Patio Installation FAQs

How much does a block paving driveway cost per metre in Glasgow?

Block paving driveways in Glasgow typically range from £75 to £130 per square metre. Factors affecting price include sub-base excavation, block style, kerb detailing, and ACO channel drainage installation.

Are porcelain patios slippery when wet?

No. Exterior porcelain slabs carry an anti-slip R11 safety rating, providing excellent traction even during heavy Scottish downpours.

How long will a professionally installed driveway last?

When built over a 150mm compacted MOT Type 1 foundation with proper drainage, a block paving or porcelain driveway will easily last 25+ years with minimal maintenance.
