Step-by-Step Trade Construction Process

Our structured installation workflow guarantees precision structural foundations, clean finish lines, and zero post-project issues.

01

Excavation & Sub-Grade Preparation

Removing existing drive surface down 250mm to solid sub-grade earth and installing geotextile membrane.

02

Sub-Base Compaction & Edging

Laying 150mm MOT Type 1 granular fill, compacted in layers with heavy kerb restraint concrete beds.

03

Block Paving / Slab Installation

Precision screeding sharp sand bedding and laying pavers in herringbone or modern stretcher patterns.

04

Silica Sanding & Sealing

Vibrating kiln-dried sand into block joints or grouting porcelain slabs with weather-resistant resin mortar.

Frequently Asked Trade Questions

How long does a block paved driveway installation take in Glasgow?

A standard 2-car block paved driveway in Glasgow or Clydebank takes approximately 3 to 5 working days from initial excavation to final joint sand compaction.

What is the benefit of porcelain patio paving over natural sandstone?

Italian porcelain slabs absorb less than 0.05% water, making them completely frost-proof, stain-resistant, anti-slip (R11 rated), and resistant to moss or algae growth compared to porous sandstone.

Do driveways require surface drainage channels in Scotland?

Yes. Under Scottish SuDS water management regulations, driveways sloping towards a public highway must have Aco channel drainage installed to direct surface runoff into soakaways or garden beds.
