OUR INSTALLATION PROCESS

How We Install Artificial Grass in Glasgow

A proper sub-base is what separates a 5-year lawn from a 25-year lawn. Here's our proven process.

STEP 01

Free Site Survey

Joe Church visits your property to measure the area, assess drainage conditions, and recommend the best grass specification for your garden and budget.

STEP 02

Excavation & Clearance

We remove existing turf, topsoil, and organic material to a depth of 75–100mm, creating a stable, level surface ready for the sub-base layer.

STEP 03

Sub-Base Compaction

We lay and compact MOT Type 1 crushed stone to 50–75mm depth — creating the firm, free-draining foundation that prevents subsidence and pooling in Glasgow's wet weather.

STEP 04

Weed Membrane

Heavy-duty weed control fabric is laid across the entire sub-base, preventing weed growth and moss penetration while maintaining excellent drainage performance beneath your synthetic lawn.

STEP 05

Artificial Grass Fitting

Premium UV-stabilised artificial grass rolls are carefully measured, cut, and secured with stainless steel nails and joining tape at seams. Infill of kiln-dried sand is brushed in to stabilise the pile and provide a natural feel underfoot.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Artificial Grass Glasgow — Common Questions

How much does artificial grass installation cost in Glasgow?

Artificial grass in Glasgow is typically priced between £45 and £85 per square metre fully fitted, including excavation, compacted MOT sub-base, weed membrane, and premium synthetic turf. A typical 40m² rear garden lawn costs approximately £2,000–£3,400 installed. We provide free no-obligation quotes for all Glasgow areas.

How long does artificial grass last in Scotland's wet climate?

High-quality UV-stabilised artificial grass with a correct sub-base will last 15 to 25 years in Scotland's climate. Our grass specifications are selected for UV resistance, frost tolerance, and excellent drainage — meaning no waterlogging even during Glasgow's heavy autumn and winter rains.

Do you install artificial grass in Clydebank and East Kilbride?

Yes — Brickscapes covers Clydebank (G81), East Kilbride, Bearsden, Milngavie, Motherwell, Dumbarton and all surrounding West Scotland areas. We offer free on-site surveys with no obligation. Call Joe on +44 7884 248274 to arrange a visit.

How do you clean and maintain artificial grass in Glasgow?

We offer a professional artificial grass cleaning service across Glasgow — including power brushing to restore pile direction, moss and algae treatment, a thorough rinse, and kiln-dried sand infill top-up. We recommend a professional clean every 12–18 months to maintain appearance and drainage performance.

Is artificial grass suitable for pets and children?

Absolutely. Our artificial grass products are non-toxic, allergen-free, and available in pet-friendly specifications with antimicrobial properties. The free-draining sub-base means pet waste drains away naturally. For children's play areas, we can also install shock-absorbing underlay beneath the turf for added safety.
